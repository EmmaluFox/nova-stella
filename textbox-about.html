Welcome to Nova Stella!

Whether you are a lifelong practitioner or a curious newcomer seeking your path, you have found your people. We are a relaxed, grassroots pagan social group and discussion forum run entirely by our members, for our members. Once a month, we gather in London to share ideas, listen to thought-provoking talks by leading authors, and explore new spiritual perspectives over a friendly pint. Recent speakers we’ve been honoured to host include Professor Ronald Hutton, John Callow, Sara Mastros and Professor Patricia MacCormack. Live talks are upstairs at The Angel Pub, 73 City Road, London EC1Y1BD (nearest tube Old Street) on the 4th Monday of each month.
